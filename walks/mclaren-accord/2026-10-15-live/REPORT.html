2The contact form does not say what happens next, and there is no direct email

Desmond, phone and desktop · Contact form ("Let's Connect")

What happened: name, email, optional phone, message, and only "We'll get back to you soon". Nothing says who reads it, how soon, or whether it adds you to a mailing list. No plain email address or phone number appeared anywhere the Walkers looked.

2When content cannot load, the site says it does not exist

Desmond, Priya · Article page and Resources

What happened: when the article data could not load, the site said "Resource Not Found", and Resources said "No articles published yet. Check back soon." A visitor whose network blocks Google's data service, or whose connection drops, would believe the article was deleted. This was seen only because MSW blocked the data service, so it is about the error message, not missing content.

Expected: say the content failed to load and offer a retry.

What in MSW got in the way (for plan B)

  1. The read-only guard blocks the site's own reads: Firestore, GraphQL, or search over POST. A read-only POST allowlist is needed.
  2. Severity floors promote MSW's own blocks and proxy failures into severity-3 console findings.
  3. The masker eats ISO dates ([masked]T15:02:29Z).
  4. Dead Parrot fails client-rendered arrivals: the two arrival looks get different page states.
  5. axe records carry no selector or snippet.
  6. LCP repeats after client-side navigation.
  7. quit replays take up to 7 minutes, and its finding count confuses Walkers.
  8. No Inspection path: a fake dossier and one journey file per guide draft.
  9. No skills, no msw report, no msw individuals.
  10. Node 22 required; the box has Node 20 (all tests still pass).
  11. A registered MCP server does not reach the running session, so walks ran as headless sessions.
  12. "Screens" in leave triggers are read as scrolls.
  13. Slow networks are invisible to the Walker.
  14. No CC walk branch existed to learn from.
