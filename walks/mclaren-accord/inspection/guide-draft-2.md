2. If a "Cookie Preferences" box appears at the bottom, press "Reject All" or "Accept All". The box should close.

4. Scroll down to "Our Team / Leadership". You should see Amber Baird, Principal, with "15+ years" of cross-industry experience in logistics, pharmaceuticals, hospitality, FinTech and retail.
5. Scroll down again. You should see Jimmy Mitchell, Principal, with 15+ years in supply chain, hospitality, financial services and technology.
