# Guide: see a PickProphets simulation and how it is made

Draft guide, compiled by hand from the Survey walk `data-enthusiast-simulations` (desktop) on 2026-10-15.
Plan A has no `msw-guide` skill, so this guide was written by the Surveyor, not generated.

1. Open the start page.
   Expected: a home page whose main heading is "Call every game." A cookie box is shown near the bottom.
2. In the cookie box, press "Reject All".
   Expected: the cookie box closes. The home page stays.
3. In the top menu, press "Play".
   Expected: a page headed "Play" with a simulation card for NBA Finals 2026, Game 6, SAS @ NYK, showing 10,000 simulated games and win odds for each team.
4. Scroll down and press the card titled "MONTA CARLO".
   Expected: a page for the NBA Matchup Lab, with San Antonio Spurs @ New York Knicks and the two team rosters.
5. Press "? how it works".
   Expected: a panel headed "How MONTA CARLO works" that explains a 1 to 20 rating scale and a possession-by-possession simulation.
