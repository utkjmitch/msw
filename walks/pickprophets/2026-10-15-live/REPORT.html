Not confirmed: probably caused by the walk server

The site reads its data from Firestore over POST requests, and read-only mode blocks every POST. The server blocked 73 Firestore reads, 56 Firestore writes and 12 usage pings. These are not reported as site defects until a walk can let Firestore reads through:

  • Leaderboard: "0 Total Participants, No data available"; on slow 3G, a spinner for over 30 seconds first.
  • Predict: "No games on the board right now". Weekly Pick'em: "No NFL games scheduled yet", a dash for the week, inert week arrows and an inert "Sign in to join".
  • Console errors on every page: Firestore connection failures, and CSP errors for www.google.com/images/cleardot.gif that Firestore loads while retrying.

Whether the site writes to Firestore for a logged-out visitor (56 blocked write requests) is worth a look by the owner.
