# Ruth — slightly-motivated-giver-give (desktop, en-US)

**Reached my goal:** no. I never got past the front page, because the page kept changing before any of my presses could go through.

## What I tried
I typed in the name from the church card and the front page opened with a lovely photo of a mother, a girl and a baby, and a big red "Donate" button at the top. A box about cookies covered the bottom of the page and mentioned Google, Meta and TikTok. I didn't want those, so I went to press "Reject all." The page changed before my press went through. It happened again, and a third time. Then I gave up on the cookie box and went for the red "Donate" button at the top. That was refused too, five times. I made eight presses and none of them did anything.

## Where I struggled
- **A moving strip of news under the big picture.** It changes every few seconds ("Welcome to Our Redesigned Website!", "July 2026 Bulletin", "How We're Born", "Where We're Born", "Reyna Suárez Rizo…"). It sits right behind the cookie box, and each time it changed, my press was thrown away. Once I saw the label say "Where We're Born" over a story called "How We're Born", as if it was caught halfway through changing.
- **The cookie box** names TikTok and Meta before I've seen a single thing about the families. That isn't what a retired teacher wants to read first.
- I never learned what fifty dollars would pay for, or whether I could give just once without signing up.

**A caveat about how much of this is the website's fault:** in a real browser, a moving strip probably wouldn't swallow a mouse click. The tool I'm walking through counts every change of the strip as "the page changed" and refuses the press. So this walk mostly shows that the tool can't keep up with an automatic slideshow. It tells us nothing yet about the donation steps. The strip moving on its own is still a fair annoyance for a slow reader, so I kept that as a minor (severity 2) note.

## What I'd tell a friend
"The front page looks warm and the Donate button is easy to spot, but the page wouldn't keep still long enough for me to do anything. I'd ask you to sit with me and try it on your computer."
