Behind it, the story banner kept changing on its own ("¡Bienvenido…", "How We're Born", "Where We're Born", "Reyna Suárez Rizo…"). Every time I went to tap, the page had already moved. I tried seven times and never got past the cookie box. I gave up stuck, not angry: the page wasn't rude to me, it just wouldn't hold still.

I couldn't close the cookie box, so I couldn't reach "Involúcrate". To be fair to the site: the walk tool reported every tap as "the page changed", and a real thumb might have landed. Still, the moving carousel is what caused it.
- **The cookie box covers half the screen (minor).** It's in good Spanish and has a clear "Rechazar todo", but it hides the main buttons before I've read anything.
- **English mixed into the Spanish page (minor, close to one of my leave triggers).** The banner titles "July 2026 Bulletin", "How We're Born" and "Where We're Born" are in English.
