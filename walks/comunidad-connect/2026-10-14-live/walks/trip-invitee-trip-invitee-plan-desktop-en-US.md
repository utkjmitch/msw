# Daniel — trip-invitee-plan (desktop, en-US)

**Reached my goal:** partly. I found what a trip includes, where it goes and that lodging is homestays, and I saw contact buttons. But after four pages I hadn't found a price, a trip length, any dates, a group size, or anything on safety or insurance, so I left.

## What I tried
I came in from my colleague's email and landed on the home page. A cookie banner covered half the screen, so I rejected it. The strip of stories under the big photo kept rotating, and the page kept changing under me before I could click. I opened "What We Do", and its menu listed "Cultural Connections", the name from the email, so I went there. The page has a lot of photos and big headings: three "pillars", four "what to expect" boxes, impact numbers, and two faculty quotes with screen-tall empty boxes next to them. Through all that I learned that trips go to Nicaragua and the Dominican Republic, that students do community projects, stay in homestays and attend sessions led by local experts, and that universities are welcome. The FAQ near the bottom was the best part, but it never covers cost, length or safety. It tells me to use "our contact page" without linking to it. At the very bottom, "Start Your Journey" has three cards. I picked "Partnership Information" on the Academic Partners card because that's me, and it took me to a page about who their partners already are. That was my fourth page with no price or length, so I left.

## Where I struggled
- **No logistics at all (major).** There was no price range, no length (7 days? 10?), no sample dates, no group size, and nothing about safety, insurance, medical support or who supervises students. I can't take a proposal to my dean without at least a rough cost and length.
- **"Partnership Information" sent me to the wrong kind of page (major).** It went to "Our Partners" under Who We Are, which only says how many partners they have. It doesn't tell a new university how to set up a program.
- **The FAQ says "reach out through our contact page" but doesn't link to it,** and the top menu has no Contact item.
- **The rotating story strip on the home page** kept changing while I tried to click, and I saw no way to pause it.
- **The "What We Do" dropdown stayed open** over the page while I read and scrolled.
- **The testimonials were hard to use.** One quote is pale text on pale blue, and there are huge empty boxes with just "AL" or "T" where photos should be. I scrolled through a full screen of blank space to reach one sentence.

## What I'd tell a friend
"The program sounds right for us: service projects, homestays, real academic content, and Georgia State and UVA have done it. But the website won't tell you what it costs, how long it runs, or how they handle safety. Skip the reading and email them through the 'Group Information' or 'Get In Touch' link at the bottom of the Cultural Connections page."
