# Ana — partner-seeker-partner (desktop, en-US)

**Reached my goal:** yes. In about four pages I learned that they take outside groups, what a group does and where, and how to send a partnership inquiry. The only thing missing is a named person to write to.

## What I tried
I arrived on the homepage. The photos looked real, but a cookie banner covered the bottom. My first four clicks on "Reject all" were refused because the news strip under the hero kept changing, and the fifth worked. I went to **Get Involved**, where the intro mentions partnering. Further down, the Travel card says I can "organize a custom experience for your academic, corporate, faith, or common interest group". That eased my worry that they only take churches or schools.

I opened Travel, which took me to the Trips page. The "What Our Trips Look Like" section shows a community clinic and a home-building week in Nicaragua and a sports exchange in the Dominican Republic. All three photos were broken. Below that, "Interested in bringing your group?" says groups can partner for a customized trip.

"Get in Touch" took me to the contact page. It lists offices in Atlanta and Managua, an email address, a phone number and a form. The topic menu includes "Partnership Opportunities" and "Service Learning Trips", and the FAQ promises a reply within 24 to 48 hours. Last, I opened Where We Work and then Nicaragua, and found named communities: Los Robles, San Esteban, San Juan del Sur and the northern highlands. I didn't type or send anything.

## Where I struggled
- The news strip under the hero rotates by itself, and the page kept shifting while I tried to click.
- The trip photos on the Trips page don't load. That's the one place I most wanted to see real groups at work.
- No one is named for partnerships or group trips, only a general inbox and phone number.
- The menu dropdowns, and the form's topic list, stayed open after I moved on and covered headings and cards.
- Tour operators aren't mentioned by name. I had to read myself into "common interest group".

## What I'd tell a friend
They do take outside groups, and the site is honest about it without a sales call. Look at Get Involved, then Trips. Use the contact form with "Partnership Opportunities" selected; they say they reply within two business days. Their roots are in rural Nicaragua, including San Juan del Sur. Just don't expect a name to address your email to.
