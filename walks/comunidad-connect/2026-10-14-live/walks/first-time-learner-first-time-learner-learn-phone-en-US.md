# Marisol — first-time-learner-learn (phone, en-US)

**Reached my goal:** yes. I can say what they do and where, and I found a way to take part without giving money. But the home page's moving story strip used up more than half my patience before I could leave that page.

## What I tried
I searched the name from my cousin's post and landed on the home page. The big headline told me straight away: health, housing, youth development and cultural connections in Nicaragua, the Dominican Republic and the United States. A cookie box covered half my screen, so I tapped "Reject all." It took several tries because the page kept changing underneath me. Then I wanted "Get Involved," but my taps kept getting refused. A strip of stories under the buttons kept changing by itself every few seconds. I tapped one of its little dots, the strip stopped, and then "Get Involved" worked. That page said I could donate, volunteer, partner or advocate. I scrolled once, and the first card was **Travel**: join a scheduled trip, or organize one for a school, church or other group. That's probably how my cousin's volunteers ended up painting a classroom. Nobody asked for my email.

## Where I struggled
- **The story strip on the home page moves by itself.** I couldn't read one story before it switched, and I couldn't get a tap to land until I tapped a dot to stop it. That is where most of my patience went (7 down to 4).
- **The cookie box covered half the screen** before I had read anything, including the "Get Involved" button. At least "Reject all" was clear.
- **Some vague wording:** "changemakers," "sustainable impact," "multiplies our impact." The Mission paragraph ("vehicles through which our purpose and approach come to life") told me nothing.
- **Small mismatch:** the home page lists three countries, including the US, but Get Involved only names Nicaragua and the Dominican Republic.

## What I'd tell a friend
"It's a group that works on health, housing and youth programs in Nicaragua and the Dominican Republic, and some in the US. You don't have to give money: you can go on one of their trips, or bring a group from church or school. Just tap the dots on the home page so the slideshow stops jumping around."

## The path
1. Tapped **Reject all** on the cookie box. It was refused three times because the page changed; the fourth try worked. The box closed and the whole home page showed: headline, Our Approach / Get Involved / Donate.
2. Tapped **Get Involved**. Refused five times because the story strip kept rotating. (I also tried **scroll down**, refused for the same reason, and opening it in a new tab, which isn't allowed on this walk.)
3. Tapped the story strip dot **Go to item 4: Where We're Born**. The strip stopped moving; same home page. (Patience fell to 4.)
4. Tapped **Get Involved**. I got the Get Involved page: "Join our community of changemakers… Nicaragua and the Dominican Republic… donate, volunteer, partner, or advocate."
5. Tapped **scroll down**. I saw "Engagement Opportunities" with a **Travel** card: "Join a scheduled trip or organize a custom experience for your academic, corporate, faith, or common interest group." The Donate card was below it.

## Success conditions
- **Can say in one sentence what the organization does and where:** met at step 1, from the home page headline once the cookie box was gone.
- **Names one way to take part that is not a donation:** met at step 5 (Travel: join a scheduled trip or organize a group trip; volunteering and advocating are also mentioned at step 4).
- **Did not have to give an email or make an account to learn this:** met. Nothing asked for either.
