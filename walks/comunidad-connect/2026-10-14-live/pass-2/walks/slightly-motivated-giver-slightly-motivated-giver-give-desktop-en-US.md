# Ruth — slightly-motivated-giver-give (desktop, en-US)

**Reached my goal:** partly. I found out what fifty dollars pays for and set up a one-time gift with no account, but because this walk is read-only, pressing "Continue to Payment" didn't send anything, so I never saw the card-details screen.

## What I tried
I typed in the name from the card and got a big, warm picture of a mother and her children. A box about cookies, Google, Meta and TikTok came up, so I pressed "Reject all". I scrolled down hoping to learn who gets helped. First I read "Our programs are the vehicles through which our purpose and approach come to life" and "Community Building × Social Impact = Resilience", which told me nothing. Further down were four real photographs: health, homes, youth and travel. That was better, but there were no amounts. I pressed the red "Donate" button. The headline, "Fund the next home, clinic visit, or season of youth programs", spoke plainly, and it said the gift was tax-deductible. Under "Choose Your Impact", "Monthly" was already picked for me. I switched to "One-Time" and chose $50, "Program Materials: training supplies, therapy materials, educational resources". Below that, two boxes were already ticked. One added $1.44 in fees, which I left because it said 100% would reach the programs. The other signed me up for news, which I unticked. The summary said $51.44, and I pressed "Continue to Payment". Nothing happened because this walk doesn't send forms.

## Where I struggled
- **Monthly was chosen for me before I chose anything.** It was a bright red button with "/mo" on every price, and "One-Time" was plain text beside it. This is exactly what I'm afraid of. I caught it only because I was watching for it.
- **Two boxes were ticked without asking me.** One quietly made my $50 gift $51.44, and the other put me on the news list. The "$1.44" line was small and grey.
- The final summary never says "one-time", so I couldn't be sure at the last moment.
- The top of the home page used words I couldn't explain to a fourth grader. The plain story, Maria's family moving from a dirt floor to a real home, was at the very bottom, in pale slanted print below the payment button.
- The strip under the big picture kept changing by itself, and its little label didn't match the words beside it.

## What I'd tell a friend
"It looks like a real group with real people. Fifty dollars buys schoolbooks and supplies, and they'll show you a family whose house they helped build. You don't have to make an account. But be careful: it starts you on *monthly* giving and ticks two boxes for you, so switch to One-Time and look at every box before you press the blue button."

## The path
1. **Reject all** on the cookie box: the box went away and I saw the home page photo with "Transforming Lives Together".
2. **Scroll down**: "Our Mission" and "Our Impact Equation", hard to understand.
3. **Scroll down**: "What We Do" with four photo cards: Together For Health, Homes For Good, Youth Development, Cultural Connections.
4. **Donate** (red button, top right): the donate page, "Fund the next home, clinic visit, or season of youth programs", "Tax-deductible under 501(c)(3)".
5. **Scroll down**: "Choose Your Impact", with **Monthly already selected** and amounts from $5/mo to $500/mo.
6. **One-Time**: it turned blue and the "/mo" went away from the prices.
7. **Program Materials $50**: that card got a red border and a tick.
8. **Scroll down**: Custom Amount, a pre-ticked "Cover transaction fees… Add $1.44", "Dedicate this donation", "Direct my gift: Where it's needed most", and a pre-ticked "Keep me updated… news".
9. **Keep me updated on the impact of my donation and Comunidad Connect news**: unticked.
10. **Scroll down**: Your Donation: Program Materials, Donation $50.00, Processing fees $1.44, Total $51.44, a blue "Continue to Payment" button, and Maria's story below.
11. **Continue to Payment**: nothing changed. The walk is read-only, so the form wasn't sent.
