## What I'd tell a friend
Survey sends and the Readout are one click away in the sidebar, and Survey sends clearly says nothing goes out until you release it. To see a role's history, don't open the role from Roles. Go Work Evolution → Company timeline → pick the client → "Role replay". Before the call, check the Readout numbers against the Roles page.
